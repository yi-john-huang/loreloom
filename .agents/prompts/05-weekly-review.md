# Weekly vault review

```text
Follow AGENTS.md, .agents/policies/vault-policy.md, and
.agents/workflows/weekly-maintenance.md.

Review window:
- <START DATE> through <END DATE>

Work read-only. Inspect notes created or materially updated in the window and
report:

- unprocessed Inbox and Source notes;
- Knowledge drafts ready for human verification;
- stale or conflicting generated Wiki pages;
- orphaned notes and broken or ambiguous links;
- Projects with no next action and Areas due for review;
- metadata or privacy problems;
- a maximum of five high-value maintenance actions.

Do not implement fixes, archive notes, promote drafts, or commit changes in
this pass. Cite exact vault paths for every finding and distinguish objective
validator failures from judgment-based suggestions.
```
