# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be a welcoming place to exchange practical knowledge-management ideas.

Participants are expected to be respectful, assume good intent while addressing impact, give actionable feedback, and avoid harassment, discrimination, doxxing, or disclosure of another person's private information.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior threatens the safety or usefulness of the community. Report conduct or privacy concerns through the repository's private security-reporting channel rather than a public issue.
